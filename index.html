Triple01

Triple01 are a rock/metal band from Lower Hutt, New Zealand. The band started in early 2021 by drummer Jesse Want and singer/rhythm guitarist Sidney Jones, with lead guitarist Nicholas McKenzie joining in late 2021. It was originally just get together and jam every weekend or second weekend but soon turned to having a catalogue of 20 + songs which got stripped down to 8 songs that will appear on their first album coming out sometime in 2026. In 2023 Triple01 performed at 2023 smokefree rockquest Wellington heats under the name 0001 and Nikita Letifov joined on bass a little while later. They performed again at rockquest in 2024 and made it into the Wellington rockquest finals in 2025. This year they will be performing again at rockquest on May 24th and will have an album coming out soon, with some demos out already.
